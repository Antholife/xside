# Contributing to XSide

Thanks for helping.

- **Issues**: use the templates (bug report, feature request, question) in the issue tracker. For a bug, include your macOS, Xcode and iOS versions and the report from *Settings > Reports & Logs* (check it for personal data first).
- **Getting started**: prerequisites, a step-by-step first change, and how to test on your iPhone without breaking the apps you already use are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
- **Pull requests**: open an issue first for anything large. Fork, branch from `main`, keep the PR focused, and fill in the checklist in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
- **Rules and how to build and test**: everything is in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) (it applies to humans and AI agents alike): English and French strings, a `CHANGELOG.md` `[Unreleased]` entry, tests, and the channel security model.
- Releases are made by the maintainer only.
